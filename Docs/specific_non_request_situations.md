# Specific Situations: Greetings, Gratitude, Responses, and Identity

This document details 5 short, direct, kid-friendly situations for each phrase in the **Greetings**, **Gratitude**, **Responses**, and **Identity** categories from `LuminangPhrases.json`.

# 1. GREETINGS

## Phrase 1: "good morning" (greetings_001)
*   **Target Phrase:** `naimbag a bigat` (Ilokano) / `maayong buntag` (Cebuano)
1. You wake up and see your mom.
2. You see your classmate in the morning.
3. You walk into your classroom.
4. You see your teacher before class.
5. You greet your sibling at breakfast.

4. You see your neighbor after school.

## Phrase 4: "hello" (greetings_004)

2. You wave to a friend on the playground.
