# Specific Situations: Requests Category

This document details 5 short, direct situations for each phrase in the **Requests** category (`requests_023` to `requests_027`). 

---

## 🚨 Phrase 1: "help me" (requests_023)
*   **Target Phrase:** `tulunganak` (Ilokano) / `tabangi ko` (Cebuano)
*   **Context:** Urgent or physical distress where you need immediate physical assistance.

### 5 Short Situations:
1.  You are carrying a tall stack of books that is about to wobble and fall.
2.  Your hands are full of groceries and the front door keeps closing on you.
3.  You are holding a heavy ladder that is starting to slide sideways.
4.  You dropped your keys behind a heavy sofa and your arm is too short to reach.
5.  You are carrying a wide dining table and cannot fit it through the doorway alone.

---

## 🤝 Phrase 2: "can you help me" (requests_024)
*   **Target Phrase:** `mabalin kadi a tulunganak` (Ilokano) / `pwede nimo ko tabangan` (Cebuano)
*   **Context:** Polite, everyday non-urgent request for physical assistance or guidance.

### 5 Short Situations:
1.  You cannot reach to put your suitcase in the overhead bus rack.
2.  You want a stranger to take a photo of you in front of a monument.
3.  You cannot open a tightly sealed jar of pickles.
4.  You cannot solve a math homework problem and want a classmate's help.
5.  You cannot carry a heavy box up the stairs by yourself.

---

## ⏸️ Phrase 3: "please wait" (requests_025)
*   **Target Phrase:** `urayennak` (Ilokano) / `hulata ko` (Cebuano)
*   **Context:** Instructing someone to pause, delay their departure, or wait for you.

### 5 Short Situations:
1.  You need to stop and tie your shoelaces while walking with friends.
2.  You are still searching for your keys as your friend waits at the door.
3.  You need to run back inside the house to grab your forgotten wallet.
4.  You are looking for your ID card inside your backpack at a security checkpoint.
5.  You are on an important phone call when someone tries to talk to you.

---

## 📦 Phrase 4: "give me {item}" (requests_026)
*   **Target Phrase:** `ikanmo man {item}` (Ilokano) / `hatagi ko {item}` (Cebuano)
*   **Context:** Asking someone to hand you a specific physical object.

### 5 Short Situations:
1.  You need a wrench while lying under a car and ask your helper to pass it.
2.  You need to pay the cashier and ask your friend to hand over your wallet.
3.  You want the salt shaker passed to you from across the dinner table.
4.  Your pen ran out of ink during an exam and you ask for a classmate's spare pen.
5.  You scraped your knee and ask your mother to pass you a band-aid.

---

## ❓ Phrase 5: "can I ask" (requests_027)
*   **Target Phrase:** `mabalin kadi agsaludsod` (Ilokano) / `pwede ko mangutana` (Cebuano)
*   **Context:** Pre-qualifying or asking permission to ask a question or get directions.

### 5 Short Situations:
1.  You want to ask a local person for directions to the train station.
2.  You want to ask a market vendor for the price of an item without a tag.
3.  You want to raise your hand to ask the teacher a question during a lecture.
4.  You want to politely interrupt a coworker to ask a quick question.
5.  You want to ask a store clerk if they have a shoe in a different size.
